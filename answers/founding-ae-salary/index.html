What startups pay a founding account executive

By Justin Weller · Published October 9, 2026 · Source: First Sales Hire Tracker, week of October 19, 2026

Short answer

As of October 2026, US startups hiring their first seller offer $240K to $300K in on-target earnings, among the 3 of 13 live postings that state it. Stated base pay runs $65K to $175K. 11 of 13 mention equity. The size of the deal drives the pay more than anything else.

What the numbers say

On-target earnings. 3 postings state on-target earnings: Rulebase, Aerovy and Lantern. The range is $240K to $300K. The midpoints are $270K, $287.5K and $300K.

Base pay. 5 postings state base pay separately: $65K to $85K (CopyCat AI), $90K to $150K (Closure), $120K to $150K (Rulebase), $150K (Lantern) and $150K to $175K (Roboto AI).

Equity. 11 of 13 mention equity. The 6 that give a range run from 0.10% to 1.00%.

The deal decides the seller. CopyCat AI pays the lowest base for one or two call closes with small insurance agencies. Rulebase pays $240K to $300K on-target for $50K to $120K+ deals with fintechs, and Lantern pays $300K on-target for conference-floor selling to large distributors.

Where. 5 allow or will consider remote work in the US. The other 8 name a city, and 6 of those 8 are in San Francisco or New York.

Who they report to. Aerovy and Rulebase say the hire reports to the CEO.

How long the jobs stay open. 4 of the 13 postings have been up for three months or longer. Arch posted its role on May 15 and Roboto AI on June 23.

Common follow-ups

How much does a founding account executive make?

In 13 first sales job postings from US startups checked in the week of October 19, 2026, the 3 that stated on-target earnings ranged from $240K to $300K. Stated base pay ran from $65K to $175K.

Do founding AEs get equity?

Usually. 11 of the 13 postings mention equity. The 6 that give a range run from 0.10% to 1.00%.

Why is founding AE pay so different from one startup to the next?

The deal decides the seller. The lowest base pay here is for one or two call closes with small insurance agencies. The highest on-target pay is for $50K to $120K+ deals with fintechs and for in-person selling to large distributors and industrial hardware makers.

Is a founding AE the same as a first Head of Sales?

No. 11 of 13 postings use a founding AE title. None of them is hiring a first Head of Sales. Rulebase calls its role a Founding GTM Lead, and Clicks Health wants a founding field sales rep who feeds the AE and writes the playbook.
