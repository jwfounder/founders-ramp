What startups pay a founding account executive

By Justin Weller · Published October 9, 2026 · Data from the week of October 6, 2026

Short answer

As of October 2026, US startups hiring their first seller offer $140K to $300K in on-target earnings, with a median midpoint of $215K, among the 5 of 11 live postings that state it. Base pay runs $65K to $150K. 8 of 11 mention equity. The size of the deal drives the pay more than anything else.

What the numbers say

On-target earnings. 5 postings state on-target earnings: Fasten Health, BlueAlpha, Toolhouse AI, Warrant and Lantern. The range is $140K to $300K. The midpoints are $150K, $165K, $215K, $225K and $300K, so the median is $215K.

Base pay. 4 postings state base pay separately: $65K to $85K (CopyCat AI), $80K to $95K (Fasten Health), $85K to $100K (Warrant) and $150K (Lantern).

Equity. 8 of 11 mention equity. The 5 that give a range run from 0.10% to 2.50%.

The deal decides the seller. CopyCat AI pays the lowest base for one or two call closes with small insurance agencies. Lantern pays $300K on-target for in-person selling to distributors with $50M or more in revenue.

Where. 4 allow remote work in the US. 4 want people in the office or on site. 2 are hybrid. 1 does not say.

Who they report to. Aerovy, Warrant and BlueHill say the hire reports to the CEO. Warrant adds a Head of Revenue.

How we got these numbers

Every week we pull US B2B software startups, roughly seed to Series A, that posted a founding, first, or first-leader sales role in the last 14 days. We open every posting to confirm it is live and that the role is the first of its kind there. We copy pay exactly as posted and don't estimate missing numbers. This sample is 11 postings dated September 24 to October 2, 2026, so treat it as a snapshot, not a survey. We update it monthly from the weekly tracker.

Common follow-ups

How much does a founding account executive make?

In 11 first sales job postings from US startups in the week of October 6, 2026, the 5 that stated on-target earnings ranged from $140K to $300K, with a median midpoint of $215K. Stated base pay ran from $65K to $150K.

Do founding AEs get equity?

Usually. 8 of the 11 postings mention equity. The 5 that give a range run from 0.10% to 2.50%.

Why is founding AE pay so different from one startup to the next?

The deal decides the seller. The lowest pay in our sample is for one or two call closes with small insurance agencies. The highest is for multi-quarter deals with manufacturers and distributors.

Is a founding AE the same as a first Head of Sales?

No. 8 of 11 postings were titled Founding Account Executive. 2 hired a first Head of Sales or GTM, and both said that leader must close deals personally before building a team.
