What startups pay a founding account executive

By Justin Weller · Published October 9, 2026 · Updated October 30, 2026 · Source: First Sales Hire Tracker, week of October 26, 2026

Short answer

As of October 30, 2026, US startups hiring their first seller state $140K to $300K in on-target earnings (4 of 16 postings) and $80K to $220K in base pay (5 of 16). Those ranges come from the 16 live postings on our First Sales Hire Tracker. The four that state on-target earnings list $140K to $160K (Fasten Health), $160K to $240K (Helium), $275K to $300K (Aerovy) and $300K (Lantern). 15 of 16 mention equity, and the 8 that give a percentage range run from 0.10% to 1.00%. The size of the deal drives the pay more than anything else.

What the numbers say

On-target earnings. 4 postings state on-target earnings: Fasten Health, Helium, Aerovy and Lantern. The range is $140K to $300K. The midpoints are $150K, $200K, $287.5K and $300K.

Base pay. 5 postings state base pay separately: $80K to $95K (Fasten Health), $90K to $150K (Closure), $125K to $220K (Clark), $150K (Lantern) and $150K to $175K (Roboto AI).

Equity. 15 of 16 mention equity. The 8 that give a percentage range run from 0.10% to 1.00%. Clark states equity as a dollar value, $60K to $200K.

The deal decides the seller. Three postings show how far apart the pay runs:

CompanyThe dealPay
Fasten HealthFirst dedicated seller of a medical records API, five-figure deals or higher$80K to $95K base, $140K to $160K on-target
Clark, several founding AE seatsNew customers plus expansion$125K to $220K base, $250K to $500K commission
LanternConference-floor selling to large distributors$300K on-target

Where. 6 allow or will consider remote work in the US. The other 10 name a city, and 9 of those 10 include San Francisco or New York.

Who they report to. Aerovy says the hire reports to the CEO. Fasten Health and Helium say the hire works directly with the CEO.

How long the jobs stay open. 5 of the 16 postings have been up for three months or longer. Arch posted its role on May 15, Roboto AI on June 23, Clark on June 26 and Magnetic on July 22.

Who writes the playbook. 11 of the 16 postings ask the first seller to write or build the sales playbook. 3 (Aerovy, Luthor and Westgate) say they hand one over.

Common follow-ups

How much does a founding account executive make?

In 16 first sales job postings from US startups on our tracker for the week of October 26, 2026, the 4 that stated on-target earnings ranged from $140K to $300K. Stated base pay ran from $80K to $220K.

Do founding AEs get equity?

Usually. 15 of the 16 postings mention equity. The 8 that give a percentage range run from 0.10% to 1.00%.

Why is founding AE pay so different from one startup to the next?

The deal decides the seller. The lowest stated base pay here, $80K to $95K at Fasten Health, is for the first dedicated seller of a medical records API. The highest on-target pay is for in-person selling to large distributors (Lantern) and to industrial hardware makers (Aerovy).

Is a founding AE the same as a first Head of Sales?

No. 14 of 16 postings use a founding AE title. None of them is hiring a first Head of Sales. Westgate calls its role Founding GTM (a founding BDR seat), and Clicks Health wants a founding field sales rep who feeds the AE and writes the playbook.
