What startups pay a founding account executive

By Justin Weller · Published October 9, 2026 · Source: First Sales Hire Tracker, week of October 15, 2026

Short answer

As of October 2026, US startups hiring their first seller offer $140K to $300K in on-target earnings, among the 3 of 8 live postings that state it. Base pay runs $65K to $150K. 7 of 8 mention equity. The size of the deal drives the pay more than anything else.

What the numbers say

On-target earnings. 3 postings state on-target earnings: Fasten Health, Aerovy and Lantern. The range is $140K to $300K. The midpoints are $150K, $287.5K and $300K.

Base pay. 3 postings state base pay separately: $65K to $85K (CopyCat AI), $80K to $95K (Fasten Health) and $150K (Lantern).

Equity. 7 of 8 mention equity. The 5 that give a range run from 0.10% to 1.00%.

The deal decides the seller. CopyCat AI pays the lowest base for one or two call closes with small insurance agencies. Lantern pays $300K on-target for conference-floor selling to large distributors.

Where. 2 allow remote work in the US. 3 want people in the office. 2 are hybrid. 1 lists three cities and does not say whether the role is remote.

Who they report to. Aerovy says the hire reports to the CEO.

Common follow-ups

How much does a founding account executive make?

In 8 first sales job postings from US startups in the week of October 15, 2026, the 3 that stated on-target earnings ranged from $140K to $300K. Stated base pay ran from $65K to $150K.

Do founding AEs get equity?

Usually. 7 of the 8 postings mention equity. The 5 that give a range run from 0.10% to 1.00%.

Why is founding AE pay so different from one startup to the next?

The deal decides the seller. The lowest base pay here is for one or two call closes with small insurance agencies. The highest is for in-person selling to large distributors and to industrial hardware makers.

Is a founding AE the same as a first Head of Sales?

No. 7 of 8 postings use a founding AE title. None of them is hiring a first Head of Sales. The eighth, Clicks Health, wants a founding field sales rep who feeds the AE and writes the playbook.
