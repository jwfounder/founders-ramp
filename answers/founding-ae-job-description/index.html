What goes in a founding AE job description?

By Justin Weller · Published October 31, 2026 · Updated October 31, 2026 · Source: First Sales Hire Tracker, week of October 26, 2026

Short answer

As of October 30, 2026, the 16 US startups on our First Sales Hire Tracker that are hiring their first seller cover the same few things in their postings. 14 of 16 use a founding AE title, 15 of 16 mention equity, 11 of 16 say the hire will write or build the sales playbook, and 6 of 16 allow or will consider remote work in the US. Pay varies the most: 7 label it as base or on-target earnings, 6 give a range with no label and 3 do not state it.

The playbook line

11 of the 16 postings ask the first seller to write or build the sales playbook. Some say it in one line:

  • Clicks Health: "Build the playbook. You are the first person doing this at Clicks."
  • Helium: "Take over our founder-led motion and turn it into a repeatable playbook."
  • Magnetic: "build the playbook the next reps run".

3 say they hand one over. Westgate writes: "The first version of the outbound motion is built and producing; you're taking it over and turning it into a machine."

The tracker puts it this way for a founder writing the post: "Whatever process you have, or don't have, is what they inherit."

How long these jobs stay open

5 of the 16 postings have been up for three months or longer. Arch posted its role on May 15, Closure in June, Roboto AI on June 23, Clark on June 26 and Magnetic on July 22.

Common follow-ups

What should a founding AE job description include?

The 16 US startups on our First Sales Hire Tracker as of October 30, 2026 most often include a founding AE title (14 of 16), equity (15 of 16), who writes the sales playbook (11 of 16 say the hire does) and where the job is based. 7 of 16 label their pay as base or on-target earnings.

Do founding AE job postings state pay?

Most give a number. 7 of 16 label it as base or on-target earnings, 6 give a range with no label and 3 do not state pay. The 4 that state on-target earnings range from $140K to $300K.

Do founding AE job postings ask the hire to write the playbook?

Most do. 11 of 16 ask the first seller to write or build the sales playbook. 3 say they hand one over, and 2 say neither.

Are founding AE jobs remote?

6 of 16 allow or will consider remote work in the US. The other 10 name a city, and 9 of those 10 include San Francisco or New York.

Where can I see current founding AE job postings?

The First Sales Hire Tracker lists US startups that posted their first sales job, with pay and what each posting asks for. It is updated weekly.
