How should a founder onboard the first sales hire?

By Justin Weller · Published October 23, 2026

Short answer

Hand them a written motion, not a login. Who buys and why now, what the first call does, what has to be true before a deal is real. Put goals on one page. Let them run the call while you sit in and say nothing. Then inspect the work every week and coach the behavior, soon and specifically.

Hiring your first AE doesn't fix founder-led sales. It freezes it. Whatever process you have, or don't have, becomes what they inherit. Onboarding is where that gets decided. Read The first hire freezes the process.

Hand them the motion on day one

Who buys, and why they'd buy this week. What the first call does. What has to be true before a deal is real. We qualify with SCALE. Every deal needs a next step someone owns, with a date. That's equal commitment. If you can't write the five questions you always ask before you call something qualified, the hire can't learn them either.

Put goals on one page

One sheet. Read in a minute. Agree what good looks like before the week starts. The cadence is from Ken Blanchard and Spencer Johnson, The One Minute Manager.

Let them run the call

Sit in and say nothing. Write down what you'd have done differently. Talk about it after, not during. The seller learns the motion. You learn what you never wrote down.

Inspect the work, then coach the behavior

Once a week, look at every open deal. Inspect the last call, not the activity count. If they hit it, praise the behavior, soon, specifically. If they miss, correct the behavior, not the person. Then back to the goals. See inspect a live deal.

How you'll know it worked

You skip a call and the week still closes. If the number only moves when you're in the room, you didn't hire a seller. You added payroll.

Common follow-ups

Should my first sales hire build the sales process?

No. The hire inherits the process you have. If you can't explain how you qualify, inspect, and advance a deal in plain language, write that first.

What should I do after onboarding?

Stay the steward of revenue and step out of the next call. Read How to move off founder-led sales.
