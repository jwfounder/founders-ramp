Fractional sales leader vs VP of Sales

Short answer

If you don't have a written, repeatable sales motion yet, a full-time VP of Sales usually walks into a blank page, and that seat is expensive to get wrong. Get the motion built first, by you with outside help, then hire a VP to scale something that already works.

The founder's hired a VP of Sales from a big-name company. Day one, the VP asks for the playbook, the comp plan, the ideal customer profile, and a team to manage. There's none of that. There's the founder's memory and a CRM nobody updates.

Six months later the VP's built enterprise process for a company that hasn't found its repeatable sale. It looks like growth. It's often a bigger version of the same empty deal.

What a VP of Sales is for

A good VP scales a motion. They hire managers, set territories and quotas, build the forecast the board trusts, and grow a team that already knows how to win. They're at their best when there's something to scale.

What you need before that

Someone has to answer the earlier questions. Who buys, and why now. What the first call does. What has to be true before a deal is real. How the first seller gets hired, paid, trained, and inspected. That's building work, and it's usually done once, well, by someone who's done it before.

Where Founders Ramp fits

We don't become your VP, and you still own the number. We build the motion with you, find and interview the first seller, install commission, training, and structure, and teach you how to inspect the work. That's Installing the team. When there's a motion that works and a team to grow, that's when a VP earns the seat.

How to decide

Can you write down how you qualify, inspect, and advance a deal? If not, you need the motion before the VP.

Are deals closing without you on the call? If not, a VP will inherit your bottleneck.

Do you have sellers for a VP to lead? If the answer is zero or one, you're hiring a manager with no one to manage.

Common follow-ups

Should an early-stage startup hire a VP of Sales?

Usually not before there's a written, repeatable motion and sellers to lead. Build the motion first; hire the VP to scale it.

When does a VP of Sales make sense?

When there's a motion that works, deals close without the founder on every call, and there are sellers for the VP to lead.
