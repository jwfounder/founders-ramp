Inspect the work

When you have sellers, you inspect deals. You coach behavior. You do not inspect hope.

What is this?

Purpose, stage, next step. If they cannot say it in a minute, it is not live.

Who cares?

Economic buyer. Champion. Who is hurt if this slips. Multi-thread or it is a story.

How they decide

Success criteria they will actually accept. Metrics. Proof. Not the slide you wish they used.

What happens next

Owner, date, what good looks like on both sides. Equal commitment, or call it early.

Did they say it?

If the buyer did not say it, it is not real. A stage click is not confirmation.

Cadence

Goals on one page. Praise and correct the behavior. Then back to the goals.

1. Goals

One sheet. Read in a minute. Agree what good looks like before the week starts.

2. If they hit it

Praise the behavior, soon, specifically. How you feel about it. Then set the next bar.

3. If they miss

Correct the behavior, soon, specifically. Not the person. Then return to the goals.

The cadence is from Ken Blanchard and Spencer Johnson, The One Minute Manager.
