How these books show up in the work

Each book keeps its author. We use it on a week when you still own the number. Not a book report. What to do on Monday.

Jocko Willink and Leif Babin

Extreme Ownership

When the raid goes wrong, the leader owns it. When it goes right, the team gets the credit. That is their book. We keep their names on it.

We say it this way: success is we. Failure is I.

The number is still yours. If the demo died, you put the wrong person in the room, or you did not inspect the work, or you treated a full calendar as a forecast. The team does not need a speech. They need you to say I. Then the next day has to look different.

Credit them in the room when a deal closes. Do not take the win as proof you are the closer. Take the miss as proof the system is yours to fix.

Buy Extreme Ownership

Anthony Iannarino

Eat Their Lunch

The deal already has a vendor. Trashing them in the room makes you the cheap option. Ask what is still hurting. Let the buyer move. You are there to attach to a problem they still have, not to win an argument about the incumbent.

Buy the book

Chris Voss with Tahl Raz

Never Split the Difference

Stay curious. Get them talking. A yes you argued them into is not a yes. If you are still pitching, you do not know what the shot is.

Mark Hunter

High-Profit Prospecting

Conversations only count if they are with people who can buy. A week of rooms with people who cannot own the number is activity. It is not a pipeline.

Matthew Dixon and Brent Adamson

The Challenger Sale

Teach them something about their own week. Then the product has a place. A tour of features is not teaching. A sentence about the problem they are already in is.

Jason Jordan and Michelle Vazzana

Cracking the Sales Management Code

Inspect the work, not the story the seller tells about the work. Calls, next steps the buyer owns, who cares. Hope is not a forecast.

Anthony Parinello

Selling to the Very Important Top Officer

Get to the person who owns the number. Everyone else is a tour. If you cannot name that person, you do not have a deal. You have a calendar.

Jamal Reimer

Mega Deal Secrets

A late deal with one thread dies. Who else has to be in the room. What paper has to move. Multi-thread or you are hoping.

Ken Blanchard and Spencer Johnson

The One Minute Manager

Goals on one page. Praise the behavior the same day. Correct the behavior the same day. Do not save it for Friday.

Stephen Covey

The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People

Circles of influence and control. Work where you can act. A week spent in the circle you cannot control is a week you gave away.
