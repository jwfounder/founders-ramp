First call

Problem-first discovery

First calls are won or lost on whether you attach to a real problem, by persona, then value. Not a tour of the product.

Founders Ramp

Attach to the real problem. Same deal. Different pain.

Contributor

Goal. Get the work done without the grind.

Need. Tools that cut the manual pile.

Pain. Too many systems. Slow decisions.

VP / manager

Goal. The team hits the number they own.

Need. A process they can inspect.

Pain. Activity that does not convert.

Founder / C-level

Goal. The company actually grows.

Need. Proof a new way of working will stick.

Pain. Too much still depends on a few people.

How

Why we do it better.

  • Map each person: goal, need, pain. Same deal. Different pain by level.
  • Before, after, metrics, proof, then how you do it better.
  • Discovery questions the whole way. Not a monologue.
  • Multi-thread from the start. Do not bet the deal on one champion.
