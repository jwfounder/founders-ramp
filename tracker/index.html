The riskiest hire you will make

Your first sales hire is the riskiest hire you will make. Get it wrong and you lose months, cash, and your belief that anyone but you can sell the product.

It usually goes wrong for one of two reasons. You hire before you can explain how you win a deal. Or you hire someone who ran a playbook somewhere else and expect them to write one for you. Whatever process you have, or don't have, is what they inherit.

Every week we pull the startups that just posted that job. Read what they pay, what they ask for, and how they describe the work. It is the closest thing to seeing how your peers are making the same call.

What stood out

  • Most want the seller close by. 6 of 8 want someone in the office or close to it, and 4 of those 6 hire in San Francisco or New York. Only Luthor and Clicks Health are open to remote.
  • The deal decides the seller. Pay runs from a $65K to $85K base for one- or two-call closes with small insurance agencies, up to $300K on-target earnings at Lantern and Aerovy.
  • Several want the playbook written while the hire closes. Aerovy, Fasten Health, Lantern, Clicks Health and MadeThis all say so. Fasten Health puts a 30/60/90-day plan right in the posting.
