The riskiest hire you will make

Your first sales hire is the riskiest hire you will make. Get it wrong and you lose months, cash, and your belief that anyone but you can sell the product.

It usually goes wrong for one of two reasons. You hire before you can explain how you win a deal. Or you hire someone who ran a playbook somewhere else and expect them to write one for you. Whatever process you have, or don't have, is what they inherit.

Every week we pull the startups that just posted that job. Read what they pay, what they ask for, and how they describe the work. It is the closest thing to seeing how your peers are making the same call.

What stood out

  • The deal decides the seller. Pay runs from a $65K to $85K base for one- or two-call closes with small insurance agencies, up to $300K on-target earnings for multi-quarter deals with manufacturers and distributors.
  • Several want the playbook written while the hire closes. Aerovy, BlueAlpha, Fasten Health and Lantern all say so. Fasten Health puts a 30/60/90-day plan right in the posting.
  • Two at once. Luthor and Warrant both sell marketing compliance software to banks, and both posted founding AE jobs within a week of each other.

How we pick these. US B2B software startups, roughly seed to Series A, that posted a founding, first, or first-leader sales role in the last 14 days. We open every posting to confirm it is live and that the role is the first of its kind there. Funding appears only when the company or a public source states it. Dates come from the posting or job board; where a board shows "days ago," we count back from the week's date.
