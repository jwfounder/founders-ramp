FAQ

Questions founders ask

The questions founders ask before they call, and the short answers.

What is Founders Ramp?

Founders Ramp helps founders build a repeatable way to sell before they hire sellers. It's Justin Weller, in the room, with a founder who still has to close.

Most founders have a real product, a real buyer, and a real reason someone should talk to them. The market still doesn't know what they do, when to call, or why now. Referrals dry up and founder-led selling becomes the bottleneck. Founders Ramp puts a sales motion under that. More about why it exists.

Who is it for?

Founders with a real product and real buyers whose sales still run through them. You might have no sellers, one seller, or five.

If the number only moves when you're on the call, it's for you. If you're about to hire your first AE, or that person is struggling, it's for you. See how we work.

What happens on the free call?

You book a call with Justin. Bring one GTM issue or one deal that went quiet. We work it on the call, and you leave with a game plan.

It isn't a discovery call about us. It's a sample of the real work on your real problem. If there's more to do after that, we'll talk about it then.

Does the first call cost anything?

No. We really just want to help. Many founders won’t need us, just a push in the right direction. We believe in forward karma. ;)

How does an engagement work?

We start with the one thing that's actually on fire. Then we tailor the work to what you need. Some engagements take a single day. Others run six months or longer.

The work usually falls into five kinds: Sales Strategy, Sales Process, Sales Management, Installing the team, and Deal & Call Coaching. Whatever the size, you'll know what you're getting before we start. See the five pieces of work.

Will you become our VP of Sales?

No. We don't become your VP. You still own the number.

We help you hire your first sales leader: a frontline manager who can grow into the VP seat. Then we build the motion with them and teach you how to inspect the work. Fractional sales leader vs VP of Sales.

When should I hire my first salesperson?

When you have a signal that the GTM strategy works and is scalable, and you can explain, in plain language, how you qualify, inspect, and advance a deal. Until then, the hire freezes whatever process you have, or don't have.

Bar for this week: write the five questions you always ask before you call something qualified. If you can't, don't hire yet. When to hire your first AE.

Should my first sales hire be a VP of Sales?

No. Make your first sales hire a talented frontline sales manager who has the potential to become your VP or CRO. It's even better if they've launched a new product before, at another startup, as an individual contributor who got promoted, or inside a larger company.

Most VPs and CROs don't have the zero to one experience you need. If they do, it's at least five years old, often ten, and you need that muscle now. So the first hire either grows into the VP or CRO seat, or at some point they step back. That's how it worked at Workiva, where Justin was a founding salesperson. The first sales hire was an excellent frontline manager. Fractional sales leader vs VP of Sales.

How do I get out of founder-led sales?

You don't get out of it so much as change roles. Write the motion first, then hire someone to run it, then step out of the next call. One founder stays the steward of revenue.

If the number only moves when you're in the room, you didn't transition. You added payroll. How to move off founder-led sales.

What is SCALE?

SCALE is how we qualify a deal: Success Criteria, Conversion Economics, Adoption Path, Lock-in & Risk, and Effortless Transaction. If you assumed one of them, the deal has a hole.

Buyers can prototype an alternative in a weekend, so "we'll build it ourselves" is often the real competitor. The five things that have to be true.

What does a good first sales call look like?

It attaches to a real problem, by persona, then value. It isn't a tour of the product.

Write the objections down before you dial. Stay curious. Never trash the other option. Problem-first discovery and what you hear on the first call.

How do I know my forecast is real?

Get five fields right on every open deal: close date, stage, amount, next step, and forecast category. Then inspect them every week.

Crushing the forecast still hurts growth if nobody saw it coming, because the company can't hire against it. 5 Green Fields and inspect a live deal.
