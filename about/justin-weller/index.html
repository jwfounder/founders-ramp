About Us

Author

Justin Weller

Founder, Founders Ramp · Tampa, FL · Updated October 9, 2026

In short

Justin Weller has spent about 30 years in enterprise technology sales, launching new markets, building sales teams from zero, and running sales and operations at founder-led companies. He was on the Founding Sales Team at Workiva. He writes everything on Founders Ramp.

Where the work comes from

TSR Wireless. Launched the Florida market.

Nextel. Rebuilt the North Florida market from about 40% of plan to number one in the country.

ADP TotalChoice. Part of the first cloud payroll at scale.

Traq Wireless. Reestablished growth for future exit. The company later merged with Tangoe.

Workiva. On the Founding Sales Team, starting from zero customers, selling SEC and financial reporting. Four years in, he was promoted to launch private company reporting, stood that team up and ran it from pre-IPO through the public company. Later he worked mid-market financial services.

Smartsheet. Stood up a Global 2000 key accounts motion in the Southeast. It failed, and he keeps that lesson in the room.

Alpha FMC. Turned around the reporting practice.

What he does now

Founders Ramp works with founders of B2B software companies who still close the deals themselves. The work is the core sale: who buys, why now, what the first call sounds like, and how to inspect a deal once there is a first sales hire.
