1 Have you closed more than a handful of deals with the same kind of buyer, for the same reason, without needing your personal network to open the door?
2 Can you write down, in plain language, how you qualify, inspect, and advance a deal so someone other than you could follow it?
3 Which sounds most like your sales today?
