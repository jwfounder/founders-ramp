The first call: find out why they'd buy

By Justin Weller · Oct 26, 2026

After a short intro, I tell the buyer the usual reasons companies buy something like this: to move faster, to lower a risk, to deliver on an AI strategy mandate, to do more with the same people, or to get a cost under control.

Then I ask, "Which of these is top of mind for you?"

The answer tells you which problem to work on. How well they answer tells you whether this person knows what's going on in the business and can make things happen.

Write the objections down before you dial. Stay curious.

  • We already use X. Good. What happens after the data leaves that system?
  • Send me information. Happy to. What would make this worth a look this week?
  • Not a priority. What is? What would have to change on the process for this to matter?

Never trash the other option. Sell what is actually different.

First calls are won or lost on whether you attach to a real problem, by persona, then value. Not a tour of the product.

Do this before your next call: write the opener for one real buyer you are working on now.
