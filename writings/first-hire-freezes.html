Common questions

Will hiring my first AE fix founder-led sales?

No. Hiring freezes the process you have, messy or missing, and the AE inherits it. Pipeline stays a black box.

How do I know I'm ready to hire a first AE?

You have a signal that the GTM strategy works and is scalable, and you can explain in plain language how you qualify, inspect and advance a deal. If you can't, you'd be multiplying confusion.

What should I do this week before I hire?

Write down the five questions you always ask before you call a deal qualified. If you can't write them, wait on the hire.
