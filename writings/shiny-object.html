Are you hawking your shiny object, or solving a problem they already have?

Hiro Founder booked a calendar hold. They booked it to get him off the phone. A calendar hold is not a deal.

He did not listen to their needs. He told them about himself.

Then he showed them his shiny objects. The objects were about him.

They already have tools. Those objects are not interesting. They are hungry.

He said what if I cut the price. Will you buy my shiny objects. They told him to get out.

Then he went and got what they needed. They opened the gate. He won the city.

Write the objections down before you dial.

Watch the last call, not the calendar. That is the inspect.
